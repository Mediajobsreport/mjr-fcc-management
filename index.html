EEO center

Plain-English steps for reporting and keeping records.

Annual public-file report

Build the report from your records

For employment units covered by the EEO program requirements, the report is due annually on the anniversary of the station’s renewal-application due date.

  • Full-time vacancies and recruitment sources
  • Source that referred the person hired
  • Interview totals and source referrals
  • Brief descriptions of outreach initiatives

Read current EEO rule § 73.2080

Applicability and recordkeeping

Confirm the employment unit

The small-unit exemption applies to units with fewer than five full-time employees. Full-time means a permanent employee with a regular schedule of 30 or more hours a week. Commonly owned stations in the same market that share an employee can make up one employment unit.

All broadcast stations file Schedule 396 with a renewal application. The mid-term review thresholds differ for television and radio.

Keep recruitment and initiative records available for FCC review. The rule describes random audits and the records stations must retain. Rule and details

This checklist is a navigation aid, not a station's completed compliance file. Official rules and station-specific FCC notices control.
